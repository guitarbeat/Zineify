drag_indicator How to Fold

How to fold

8 pages · 1 sheet · 1 cut

  1. 1Design your zine on one sheet, with the pages in this order and orientation.
  2. 2Crease the sheet along both main directions — fold in half each way, then open flat.
  3. 3Make two more creases to divide the long direction into quarters, then open flat again.
  4. 4Fold in half, then cut along the center crease — only as far as the quarter-fold marks.
  5. 5Open it out flat. You'll see a short slit through the center.
  6. 6Fold in half along the long direction so all pages stay on the outside.
  7. 7Push inward from both ends — the slit opens into a cross. Bring pages together with cover outermost.
  8. 8Fold flat and crease. You now have an 8-page booklet.
