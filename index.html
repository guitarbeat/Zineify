How to fold
  1. 1Print single-sided at 100% scale.
  2. 2Fold in half widthwise, crease, and unfold.
  3. 3Fold outer edges to center crease to make 8 panels.
  4. 4Fold in half again and cut along the center crease between the inner panels.
  5. 5Unfold completely flat.
  6. 6Fold lengthwise (hotdog style) with printed side facing out.
  7. 7Push outer ends inward so the center slit opens into a diamond.
  8. 8Wrap pages around and press flat into your 8-page booklet.
